Create a fun, colorful, polished and highly memorable website for **Nether Ninjas**, a Minecraft-focused creative studio.

# ABOUT NETHER NINJAS

**Nether Ninjas** is a Minecraft content studio creating experiences for players of all ages, with a particular focus on younger audiences.

We create projects across three main areas:

* **Minecraft Realms**
* **Minecraft Marketplace**
* **Minecraft Education**

Our goal is to create fun, accessible and memorable Minecraft experiences that encourage players to explore, play, learn and create.

The website should communicate that Nether Ninjas is a professional studio, while still feeling playful, welcoming and full of personality.

---

# CORE AUDIENCE

The final experiences created by Nether Ninjas are primarily designed for **children and younger players**.

Because of this, the website should feel:

* Fun
* Friendly
* Colorful
* Playful
* Creative
* Safe
* Approachable
* Exciting
* Easy to understand

However, the website should NOT look childish or amateur.

It should feel like a **professional creative studio that happens to make joyful Minecraft experiences for younger audiences**.

Parents, educators, publishers and potential business partners should also feel that the company is professional and trustworthy.

---

# BRAND COLORS

The main brand colors are:

**Purple**
Primary brand color.

**White**
Used for clean backgrounds, cards and contrast.

**Black**
Used for typography, outlines and strong visual contrast.

Purple should be the dominant accent throughout the website.

Use different shades of purple where appropriate, but keep the overall palette centered around:

Purple + White + Black

Avoid making the entire website dark.

The website should primarily use bright white and light backgrounds, with purple sections and black accents.

---

# VISUAL STYLE

The visual identity should be:

**Cartoon + Minecraft + Modern + Playful**

Use inspiration from modern children's games and polished Minecraft experiences.

Important visual characteristics:

* Thick rounded shapes
* Soft corners
* Cartoon-style illustrations
* Playful typography
* Bold headings
* Black outlines
* Purple accents
* Floating elements
* Minecraft-inspired shapes
* Cute decorative details
* Small animated elements
* Friendly icons
* Large screenshots of Minecraft worlds

The design should feel alive.

Avoid:

* Corporate minimalism
* Extremely dark gaming aesthetics
* Realistic military/game studio aesthetics
* Excessive gradients
* Overly complicated interfaces
* Generic stock illustrations

---

# ANIMATIONS

Animations should make the website feel alive without becoming distracting.

Use:

* Smooth scrolling
* Cards slightly moving on hover
* Images gently scaling
* Buttons with playful movement
* Floating decorative elements
* Small particles
* Scroll reveal animations
* Subtle parallax effects
* Cartoon elements moving slightly

For example, when hovering over a project card:

The image slightly zooms in.

The card moves upward by a few pixels.

The arrow moves to the right.

The project title remains clearly readable.

---

# MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Add small details throughout the site.

Examples:

A small purple sparkle appears when hovering over important buttons.

Project cards can have tiny animated Minecraft-inspired particles.

Navigation buttons can slightly bounce or move.

Section illustrations can have subtle floating animations.

Keep everything smooth and polished.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use a bold, rounded and playful display font for headings.

Body text should use a clean, highly readable modern sans-serif.

The typography should feel friendly and accessible.

Avoid overly futuristic or aggressive gaming fonts.

Headings should be bold and large.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Because the website represents a company whose experiences are designed for younger audiences:

* Keep text easy to understand.
* Maintain strong contrast.
* Use clear buttons.
* Avoid excessive text walls.
* Use clear navigation.
* Make interactive elements obvious.
* Make the website fully responsive.

---

# RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Desktop:

Large illustrations, cinematic project cards and playful layouts.

Tablet:

Adapt grids and spacing.

Mobile:

Single-column layouts.

Large touch-friendly buttons.

Simple navigation.

Optimized images.

No horizontal scrolling.

---

# IMPORTANT BRAND RULES

The website should consistently use:

**PURPLE + WHITE + BLACK**

The visual identity should be:

**CARTOONY + MINECRAFT + PLAYFUL + PROFESSIONAL**

Do NOT make it:

* Dark and intimidating
* Corporate
* Generic
* Too childish
* Overly realistic
* Full of Minecraft textures
* Similar to a Minecraft server website

The website should look like a **real creative studio website made for a young audience**.
